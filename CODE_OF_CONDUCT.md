# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be considerate,
assume good faith, discuss technical trade-offs with evidence, and respect
privacy—especially when handling security reports.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
doxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, sustained disruption,
or retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. For a
confidential conduct concern, contact a maintainer privately through the same
channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), clearly labeling it as a code
of conduct report.

Maintainers will avoid conflicts of interest and protect reporter privacy as
far as practical. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented
privately, and communicated to the affected participants.
